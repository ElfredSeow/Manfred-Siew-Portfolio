Where this starts

I trained on airframes before I trained on software.

I learned riveting, soldering and composite repair at the Lufthansa Technical Training Center as part of World Skills aircraft-maintenance training — work where a missed inspection is not a bug report. That is where the habit came from: read the manual, check the tolerance, sign your name to it. I build software the same way, and the rest of this page is the climb out of the hangar.
