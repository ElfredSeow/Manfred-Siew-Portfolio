# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and technical evaluators (RSAF units, Temasek Polytechnic, freelance clients) assessing Manfred Siew for aerospace/IT and AI-engineering roles. They skim fast and compare many candidates, so they need to grasp breadth (27 projects across categories/orgs) and depth (case studies) quickly.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio positioning Manfred as trained in Aerospace Engineering and self-taught in software — both disciplines combined, not a pivot away from aerospace — aimed at AI engineering roles. Success is a reviewer quickly understanding the range of his work and taking the contact CTA.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, smooth, round. One signature element per section; radius as a system; nothing decorating a 90° corner. Confident and precise — engineering-credible, not trend-chasing or SaaS-generic.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS/AI-startup templates: gradient text, glassmorphism, hero-metric blocks, an eyebrow on every section. Also avoid literal travel-site tourism aesthetics (busy stock photography, saturated review badges) when borrowing UX patterns — take the interaction pattern (inviting, tactile horizontal gallery), not the genre.

## Design Principles

- Radius as a system: one consistent rounding scale, no sharp corners as decoration
- One signature element per section, not stacked flourishes
- Precision over decoration — every visual choice should read as engineered, not templated
- Aerospace + IT dual identity should be legible without over-explaining
- Category imagery is abstract (gradient + icon per hue), not photographic — there's no photo of "process automation"

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum. Reduced-motion fallback required (site already handles `prefers-reduced-motion`). Touch/click targets should be generous — the category gallery currently fails this (cards partly hidden, hard to scroll and click).
